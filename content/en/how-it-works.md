## In short

With {{< brand >}} you have two relationships with the cooperative: you are a **member** (you are part of it and take part in the members' assembly) and a **worker** (you are employed and receive a payslip). This is the worker-member model set by Italian Law no. 142 of 3 April 2001.

Your client, in Italy or abroad, signs with the cooperative rather than with you. It receives a regular invoice and pays the cooperative, which then pays your salary, contributions, insurance and its own service fee.

## Steps

1. **First contact** via the [form](/en/contact/) or WhatsApp: what you do, who the client is, duration and approximate value.
2. **Check.** We confirm the activity can be handled (no professions reserved to registered professionals) and which employment form fits.
3. **Membership.** You apply, subscribe the membership share and sign online.
4. **Client contract.** We draft the contract with your client, in English if needed: scope, timing, price, payment, IP, confidentiality.
5. **Work.** You work as usual and log activity and expenses on the platform.
6. **Invoice and collection** under the applicable VAT rules, in foreign currency too.
7. **Salary.** Monthly payslip; at year end the CU tax statement for your tax return.

## How the contract value is split

From the billed amount (excl. VAT) we deduct the cooperative's service fee (see [pricing](/en/pricing/)). The rest is your **activity budget**: it covers refundable expenses, employer contributions and taxes, and your gross salary, on which employee contributions and withholding apply. Before you start we show you the calculation for your case, line by line.

## Who it is not for

- Professions reserved to members of a professional register.
- Activities requiring licences the cooperative does not hold.
- People living abroad permanently, unless tax residence and social security have been checked first.
- Companies looking for staff to fold into their own organisation: we do not place people with companies that manage them as their own employees. In that case we recommend direct hiring.

## What stays with you

Finding clients, setting the price, doing the work. The cooperative does not assign you clients and does not take away your professional autonomy with the clients you already know.
