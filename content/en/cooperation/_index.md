A worker cooperative is not an intermediary: it is a business owned by the members who work in it. This section explains how cooperation works (mutualism, the worker-member, Italian Law 142/2001, internal regulations, the members' meeting, governance, patronage refunds) and how you actually work with your own employment contract, profession by profession and situation by situation. Examples with people are fictional and only illustrate the mechanism.
