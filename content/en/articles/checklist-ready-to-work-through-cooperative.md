Before signing up, it pays to stop for ten minutes and take stock. This checklist does not give a verdict: it helps you see what you already have clear and what remains to be defined. Answer honestly, note the "don't knows" and bring them to the conversation.

## 1. Your activity

- [ ] I can describe in two sentences **what I do** for the client.
- [ ] My work is **a service activity**, mostly remote or with a clear location.
- [ ] It is not a profession requiring **registration with a professional body with exclusive rights** (lawyer, doctor, registered engineer and the like).
- [ ] If I produce creative works or am a sales agent, I know **specific rules apply** and need checking.

## 2. The client

- [ ] I have a **client** or a concrete proposal (possibly foreign).
- [ ] I know **who decides** at the client and who handles contracts and payments.
- [ ] I have a **draft agreement**, or at least outline terms (duration, commitment, rate).
- [ ] I know the client's **country and currency**.
- [ ] The client is **willing to sign** with a cooperative.

## 3. The working relationship

- [ ] I understand I will be a **member** with an employment relationship with the cooperative (normally as an employee).
- [ ] I know I **do not open a VAT number** for this activity and do not keep accounts.
- [ ] I have considered what happens with **other jobs** or the VAT number I already have.
- [ ] I am aware of the **member share** and its conditions.
- [ ] I have read or requested the **by-laws and regulation**.

## 4. Money and timing

- [ ] I have calculated the **minimum rate** that covers labour and service costs.
- [ ] I know **net pay** depends on many factors and should not be taken for granted.
- [ ] I know the client's **payment terms**.
- [ ] I have asked how **advance and payslip timing** work.
- [ ] I have a **reserve** for any initial delays.

## 5. Organisation

- [ ] I have a **workspace** and suitable equipment.
- [ ] I am willing to **record hours and activities** regularly.
- [ ] I can handle **time zones** and remote communication.
- [ ] I have considered **confidentiality and intellectual property** of the work.

## 6. Documents

- [ ] ID document and tax code.
- [ ] Personal IBAN.
- [ ] CV or description of your activity.
- [ ] Draft of the client's contract, if it exists.

## How to read the results

If you ticked almost everything, you are ready for a practical conversation. If you have many "don't knows" about the client or costs, spend a few days gathering information. If you have doubts about your profession, clarify that point first.

## Example: Carlo, IT consultant

A fictional example: Carlo fills in the checklist and finds three open points: he does not know whether the client accepts a cooperative, has not calculated the minimum rate and has not read the regulation. He explains the solution to the client (who accepts), runs the numbers with the cooperative's help and reads the regulation, noting two questions. He arrives at the interview with a clear picture and admission goes smoothly.
