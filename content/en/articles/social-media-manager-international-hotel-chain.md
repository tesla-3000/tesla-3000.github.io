A chain of boutique hotels with properties in Italy, Greece and Portugal wants a single direction for social media, but has its marketing team in London. It looks for someone who knows the Italian destinations, writes in several languages and can react when a post goes viral or a guest leaves an unhappy comment. The assignment is typically continuous, with seasonal peaks.

## What the work includes

- **Monthly editorial calendar**, tied to local events, seasons and promotions.
- **Content production**: photos and short videos, captions in several languages, stories and reels.
- **Community management**: replies to comments and messages, flagging problems to reception.
- **Collaborations** with creators and micro-influencers hosted at the properties.
- **Monthly report** with reach, engagement and booking enquiries coming from the profiles.

Seasonality is decisive: in April you prepare summer, in September winter holidays, and peak-month activity requires presence at weekends too.

## Points to clarify in the contract

1. **Approvals**: who approves posts and within how long. A content piece blocked for days loses its moment.
2. **Crisis handling**: negative review, controversy, price error. You need a protocol and a contact person.
3. **Weekend availability**: include it in pay or pay it separately.
4. **Accounts**: profiles must be registered to the hotel; the professional accesses with roles and permissions.
5. **Trips to the properties**: for visits and shoots, expenses reimbursed.

## Handling seasonality in an orderly way

Prepare with the client an annual calendar with the big dates (openings, events, holidays) and the campaigns needing visual material. Shoot in good light and at low occupancy, and gather more content than needed in a single visit. In high season agree a contact at the property for quick information on offers and availability. Document in a short manual the standard replies to frequent comments, so whoever covers your days off keeps the same tone.

## How it works with the cooperative

1. **Present the assignment**: properties, languages, calendar, type of content.
2. **Membership** with a share of capital refundable on exit (Law 142/2001).
3. **Contract** with the chain: approvals, crises, availability, reimbursements.
4. **Platform**: activities, trips, expense claims, signed documents.
5. **Invoicing** the chain, in pounds too, under B2B rules verified case by case.
6. **Pay**: payslip with INPS, INAIL, holidays and sick leave under the employment relationship; professional liability included.
