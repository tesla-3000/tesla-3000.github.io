A Boston tech company wants to build an engineering team in Italy, an Israeli scale-up needs a sales lead in Milan. They have no local HR and do not know the Italian labour market: typical salaries, notice periods, candidate expectations. They need an HR consultant or recruiter with a network and an understanding of local habits. For the professional the assignment can be an opportunity, but the contract must reflect the nature of the work.

## The recruiter's work in short

Typical phases of a search:
- **briefing** with the company: profile, level, skills, location, expectations;
- **sourcing**: active search, direct contact, ads, word of mouth;
- **screening** by phone and interview;
- **shortlist** of three to five candidates with an evaluation sheet;
- **interviews with the client** and feedback handling;
- **offer and negotiation**, with attention to Italian notice periods;
- **follow-up** in the first months after joining.

A search takes on average six to twelve weeks. Deliverables are the shortlist, evaluation sheets and a market report.

## Pay models

There are three main ones. **Retainer** (part on start, the rest on shortlist delivery), **success fee** (paid only if the candidate is hired) and **hourly or monthly consulting**. The success model is common but risky: if the company changes its mind after six weeks, the work done goes unpaid. An advance and a clause on the guarantee period (what happens if the candidate resigns early) make the contract balanced.

## Mind the boundaries

Selecting candidates for a client is different from handling their hiring or employment relationship. The consultant handles the search; for onboarding into the company, the client must choose the instrument and deal with it separately. Also, processing CVs is subject to privacy law: candidates need clear notices.

## Measuring the success of a search

Agree with the client process indicators and not only the final hire: time to first shortlist, number of qualified candidates, interview acceptance rate. Keep a contact log with date and source, useful also for complying with privacy rules. Explain Italian habits early to the client, such as long notice periods and the weight of contract security, to avoid uncompetitive offers. Timely feedback to unselected candidates protects the reputation of both searcher and hirer.

## How it works with the cooperative

1. **Present the search**: roles, timing, pay model.
2. **Membership** with a share of capital refundable on exit (Law 142/2001).
3. **Contract** with retainer, bonus, guarantee and data handling.
4. **Platform**: activities, expenses for interviews and ads, documents.
5. **Monthly payslip** with INPS, INAIL, professional liability included.
6. **Invoicing** the foreign client, in foreign currency too, with B2B rules verified case by case; advance possible.

## Cooperative or VAT number

Independent consultants with many mandates often use a flat-rate VAT number (85,000 euro threshold, 15% substitute tax, 5% in the first five years with requirements), with Separate Management contributions and their own invoices. It is flexible and suits those looking for several clients. In the cooperative the assignment is framed as an employment relationship with ordinary protections, suited to those dedicating themselves to one client at a time.
