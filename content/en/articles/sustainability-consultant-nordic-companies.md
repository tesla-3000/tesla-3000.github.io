Companies in Northern Europe are often further ahead in sustainability reporting: ESG reports, supply-chain analysis, emissions calculation, preparation for new disclosure duties. Many look for external consultants with specific skills, including from abroad. For an Italian consultant this is an opportunity, but it needs a clear contractual set-up.

## Why this consulting suits a cooperative

The typical assignment is project-based: data mapping, analysis, drafting a report, training internal teams. It lasts weeks or months, with milestones, and the client wants a supplier that invoices regularly and is reliable. {{< brand >}} gives the client a single commercial contract and the consultant an employment relationship (Law 142/2001) with payslips, INPS contributions, INAIL coverage, holidays and TFR.

Note: regulated professions with exclusive rights cannot be handled this way. Sustainability consulting normally is not one, but if your assignment includes activities reserved to a professional register, we check first.

## Example: Francesca and a Danish company

Example: imagine Francesca, an ESG reporting specialist, who gets an assignment from a Danish manufacturer to set up its first sustainability report in four months. She works from home with a few trips to Copenhagen. She joins the cooperative.

The cooperative signs the contract with the manufacturer: objectives, deliverables, schedule, confidentiality and payment by progress milestones. Francesca logs activities on the platform, claims travel expenses and receives a payslip. When the report is delivered the engagement closes, but she can remain a member for a new assignment.

## How it works with the cooperative

1. **Present the assignment.** You tell us client, scope, duration and deliverables.
2. **Activity check.** We confirm it is not a regulated profession with exclusive rights.
3. **Membership.** You subscribe the share capital quota.
4. **Commercial contract.** The cooperative signs it, including confidentiality, ownership of deliverables and payment schedule.
5. **Employment relationship.** We define your engagement for the assignment.
6. **Delivery.** You log activities, upload documents and ask for support when needed.
7. **Invoicing and collection.** We invoice the foreign client, including in foreign currency; a possible advance through the fund.

## Points to look after

**Confidentiality.** Nordic clients care about data handling. The contract should include confidentiality clauses and state who accesses which information.

**Milestone payments.** On long assignments, agree payment by progress milestones; the cooperative invoices accordingly and can consider an advance.

**Invoicing.** For B2B services to EU clients the reverse-charge mechanism normally applies; for non-EU clients the transaction is generally not subject to Italian VAT. We check each case without generalising.

**Residence.** If you work from another country, tax residence and double taxation treaties must be assessed separately.

We promise no net amounts: they depend on personal circumstances, with income tax applied through withholding at source and year-end adjustment.

## Language, standards and keeping current

With Nordic companies English is the working language, but regulatory references change fast: it helps to state in the contract which version of standards and duties the report refers to. The members' community and the training offered by the cooperative can help you stay current and compare notes with colleagues on similar cases.

## Rates and scope of the assignment

In sustainability consulting the risk is constant scope creep: a client asks for a report, then a supplier mapping, then a training plan. Define in the contract the scope, the number of included revisions and how extra requests are quoted. You set the rate with the client; the cooperative checks it works for the employment relationship and formalises it.

Finally, keep a simple log of assumptions and data sources used in each report: Nordic clients and their auditors often ask how a figure was obtained, and a clear trail saves time and protects your professional reputation.
