Worker cooperative for freelancers in Italy

Your client is abroad. Your payslip is in Italy.

Become a member of the cooperative, get hired, and keep working for your clients as you do today. Client contract, invoices, collections, contributions and payroll are on us. No VAT number, no accountant.

Become a member How it works

How it works, in four steps

  1. Tell us about your workWho the client is, what you do, what the contract is worth. We reply on WhatsApp or email.
  2. Become a memberSubscribe the membership share and sign online.
  3. We sign with your clientThe cooperative signs the contract, in English if needed, and invoices the client.
  4. Get paid monthlyA monthly payslip with INPS pension contributions, INAIL insurance, paid leave, sick pay and severance (TFR).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Italian VAT number (partita IVA)?

No. The cooperative invoices your client and you are employed as a worker-member with a payslip.

Do I choose my clients and my rate?

Yes. You bring the client and agree the price; the cooperative signs the contract and handles the admin.

Do I need to live in Italy?

Usually yes. If you live abroad, tax residence and social security rules must be assessed case by case before starting.
