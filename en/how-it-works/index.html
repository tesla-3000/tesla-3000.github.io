How the cooperative works

In short

With Tesla3000 you have two relationships with the cooperative: you are a member (you own part of it and vote) and a worker (you are employed and receive a payslip). This is the worker-member model set by Italian Law no. 142 of 3 April 2001.

Your client, in Italy or abroad, signs with the cooperative rather than with you. It receives a regular invoice and pays the cooperative, which then pays your salary, contributions, insurance and its own service fee.

Steps

  1. First contact via the form or WhatsApp: what you do, who the client is, duration and approximate value.
  2. Check. We confirm the activity can be handled (no professions reserved to registered professionals) and which employment form fits.
  3. Membership. You apply, subscribe the membership share and sign online.
  4. Client contract. We draft the contract with your client, in English if needed: scope, timing, price, payment, IP, confidentiality.
  5. Work. You work as usual and log activity and expenses on the platform.
  6. Invoice and collection under the applicable VAT rules, in foreign currency too.
  7. Salary. Monthly payslip; at year end the CU tax statement for your tax return.

How the contract value is split

From the billed amount (excl. VAT) we deduct the cooperative’s service fee (see pricing). The rest is your activity budget: it covers refundable expenses, employer contributions and taxes, and your gross salary, on which employee contributions and withholding apply. Before you start we show you the calculation for your case, line by line.

Who it is not for

  • Professions reserved to members of a professional register.
  • Activities requiring licences the cooperative does not hold.
  • People living abroad permanently, unless tax residence and social security have been checked first.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of employment contract do I get?

You are a worker-member under Italian Law 142/2001, with an employment relationship with the cooperative, usually as an employee, shaped on the length and type of the client contract.

Who pays social security?

The cooperative, as employer, pays INPS contributions and the INAIL premium; they are covered by the contract value.
