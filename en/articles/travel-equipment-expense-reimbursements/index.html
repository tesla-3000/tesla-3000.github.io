Reimbursement of travel and equipment expenses

Anyone working for foreign clients spends money: tickets, accommodation, meals on business trips, materials for a project, sometimes software or equipment. For a freelancer these are costs deducted or built into the rate. With Tesla3000, expenses linked to the engagement follow a clear path: expense claim, check, reimbursement.

Which expenses can be reimbursed

The general rule: expenses incurred to carry out the engagement and provided for in the commercial contract or regulation are reimbursable. Typically:

  • transport: train, plane, car hire, taxi where provided;
  • accommodation and meals during the trip;
  • materials specific to the project;
  • equipment or software bought for the engagement, if agreed in advance.

Not everything you spend while working is reimbursable. Personal expenses, general-use equipment or anything not provided for fall outside, barring specific agreements. That is why the right time to clarify is before, not after.

Example: Martina on assignment in Prague

Example: imagine Martina, a training consultant, who has to deliver a three-day workshop in Prague for a Czech client. The engagement provides that travel and accommodation be reimbursed with agreed ceilings. Martina books, keeps tickets and receipts, and after the trip uploads the expense claim on the platform with the supporting documents. The cooperative checks them against the rules and reimburses. For the purchase of a portable projector, which was not provided for, Martina had asked for authorisation first: since it had not been agreed, it would not have been reimbursed automatically.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Definition in the engagement. It sets which expenses are reimbursable, with any ceilings and whether the client pays directly.
  2. Advance authorisation. For significant expenses you ask for confirmation before buying.
  3. Keeping receipts. Invoices, receipts and tickets with legible details.
  4. Expense claim. You complete it on the platform, with dates, amounts and attachments.
  5. Check. The cooperative verifies consistency with the engagement and rules.
  6. Reimbursement. The amount is paid as provided, and expenses are tracked in the documents.

Expenses advanced by the client

In many cases the most convenient solution is for the client to book and pay flights and hotels directly, or reimburse expenses to the cooperative separately, with a separate invoice. That way the member does not have to advance large sums. It is something to agree in the contract: who advances, within what limits, in what timeframe. If the expense is high and the client pays at 60 days, an advance through the fund may be worth considering.

Tax aspects

The tax treatment of expense reimbursements depends on the type of expense, the documentation and the employment relationship: for business trips, for instance, there are specific rules. We do not go into detail or run simulations; the cooperative applies the rules provided. For expenses abroad, keep receipts even if in other languages or currencies and always note date and exchange rate when asked.

Common pitfalls

Three mistakes cause most problems. First, buying before asking: an unplanned expense may not be reimbursable. Second, losing receipts: a card statement alone is often not enough, so keep the original document and photograph it at once. Third, mixing personal and work costs on the same receipt, such as a meal with a friend during a trip. Keeping claims tidy and submitting them promptly, ideally within a few days of the trip, makes checking faster and reimbursement smoother, and avoids discussions months later.

Foreign receipts

Receipts in other languages are fine, but add a short note on what the expense was for, the date and the currency, so that whoever checks the claim does not have to guess. If the amount is in a foreign currency, record the exchange rate used by your card or bank as shown on the statement, and attach it where possible.

Frequently asked questions

Which expenses are reimbursed?

Those provided for by the engagement and the regulation, with adequate documentation. The concrete list is explained before the activity starts.

How do I submit expenses?

Through an expense claim on the platform, attaching receipts and invoices.

Is personal equipment reimbursable?

It depends on what the engagement and regulation provide: specific purchases for a project can be agreed beforehand; general-use equipment normally remains your responsibility.
