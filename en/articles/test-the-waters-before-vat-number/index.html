Advantages

Testing the waters before opening a VAT number

Moving from employment or study to freelancing is a big decision. You open the VAT number and only afterwards find out whether clients come, whether the price is right, whether you really like working alone. With Tesla3000 you can try first, with a safety net, and decide with more information.

The problem of the leap into the dark

Opening a VAT number involves immediate choices: tax regime, social security scheme, invoicing software, perhaps an accountant. Then there are fixed costs and deadlines, even when clients are few. If the project does not take off, closing requires further paperwork. The risk is less financial than organisational and psychological: many people give up for fear of getting it wrong.

The cooperative offers an in-between path: you work for real clients, with an employment relationship, payslips and protections, without having to build the administrative infrastructure from scratch.

What you can test

  • The market. You find and manage clients, including foreign ones, and check whether there is demand for what you offer.
  • Your rate. You negotiate fees with clients and see what they are willing to pay.
  • Work rhythm. You learn how much work you can handle alone, with what quiet periods.
  • Preferences. You find out whether you prefer project work, ongoing clients or team collaboration.
  • Soft skills. Time management, communication, quoting.

Example: Alessio and the first six months

Example: imagine Alessio, a developer who has been an employee for five years. He would like to go freelance but fears the uncertainty. He joins the cooperative and, with a first American client found through a contact, starts an engagement. He logs activities, receives a payslip and learns to estimate timing. After six months he has two clients and has understood that he likes project work, but also that he misses a team. He decides to stay in the cooperative and collaborate with other members. Someone else in his place might have concluded they prefer a VAT number: in both cases the choice is informed.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Call. You tell us what you want to do and with which clients.
  2. Membership. You subscribe the share capital quota, refundable on exit under the by-laws.
  3. First engagement. The cooperative signs the contract with the client and we define your employment relationship.
  4. Day to day. Activities, expense claims and payslips on the platform, with WhatsApp support.
  5. Review of the experience. After a few months you assess results, clients and preferences.
  6. Choice. You continue in the cooperative, join a team or decide to open your own VAT number.

What to keep in mind

Testing the waters does not mean everything is easy: if clients do not come, the employment relationship still depends on engagements and the cooperative’s rules. There is no guarantee of work. Also consider that some activities, such as regulated professions with exclusive rights, cannot be handled in a cooperative.

If you later decide to leave, withdrawal follows the by-laws and regulation; we explain beforehand how it works and how to recover your share. Moving to a VAT number also needs preparation: tax regime, pension scheme, clients. We note that for the flat-rate VAT regime the threshold is 85,000 euro and the substitute tax 15%, reduced to 5% in the first five years with the requirements.

Set simple goals for the trial

To get real information from the experience, decide in advance what you want to learn: how many clients you can find in three months, what daily rate they accept, how many hours a week you can bill. Note the results at the end of each month. A short written review will tell you far more than a general impression, and it will give you solid numbers for deciding whether to continue, grow in the cooperative or open your own VAT number.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open a VAT number later?

Yes. At any time you can decide to leave the cooperative and open your own VAT number. Withdrawal follows the by-laws and regulation.

How long does testing take?

There is no fixed time: it depends on your goals and clients. Some use a few months, others longer.

Is the member's share refundable?

Yes, on exit, as provided by the by-laws.
