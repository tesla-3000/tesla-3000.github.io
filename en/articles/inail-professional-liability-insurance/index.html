Advantages

INAIL and professional liability insurance for members

Anyone working independently knows that mishaps can be costly: an accident that stops activity, a professional error the client disputes. Two insurance tools respond to different risks and are worth not confusing: INAIL and professional liability. With Tesla3000 both are part of the protections framework for members.

INAIL: accidents and occupational illness

INAIL is the compulsory insurance against workplace accidents and occupational diseases. In an employment relationship in the cooperative, cover is provided: the cooperative pays the premium and handles the reports. If you suffer a workplace accident, or one on the commute in the cases the rules provide, benefits are those set by law. We do not go into amounts or durations: they depend on the case.

For people working at a computer it may seem irrelevant, but accidents can happen anywhere: a crash on the way to a client, a fall during a business trip. For those doing more physical work, such as set building, installations or events, cover matters even more. Workplace safety, including training, is a subject the cooperative considers for members’ activities.

Professional liability: errors and damage to third parties

Professional liability protects against compensation claims by a client or third parties for damage resulting from errors in the work performed: wrong advice, a substandard delivery, data loss. It is a different cover from INAIL, because it does not protect you physically but the assets of the cooperative and the member from the financial consequences of a mistake.

Professional liability insurance is included in the members’ package. Each policy has ceilings, excesses and exclusions: it does not cover everything and does not replace professional diligence. We provide the conditions before you join, so you can compare them with what your clients require.

Example: Federico and the project in Denmark

Example: imagine Federico, an audio-video technician working on an installation in Denmark. During assembly he slips off a ladder and is hurt: it is a workplace accident, which the cooperative reports as the procedure requires. Two months later the client disputes a problem with the installation and asks for compensation: if the claim falls within the policy conditions, the cooperative activates the professional liability cover. Federico faced two different events with two different covers.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Before joining. You receive information on the covers and policy conditions.
  2. Employment contract. Your relationship includes INAIL cover, paid by the cooperative in an employment relationship.
  3. Activities. You log activities and workplaces on the platform, useful for classifying risks.
  4. If you have an accident. You tell the cooperative at once and hand over medical documents: reports have tight deadlines.
  5. If the client raises a dispute. You tell the cooperative at once, without admitting liability or replying alone.
  6. Handling. The cooperative reviews next steps with you and the insurer.

What to keep in mind

Covers have limits. Insurance does not cover deliberate breach, undeclared activities or damage above the ceilings. If you work abroad, check whether cover extends to the country where you operate and whether that client requires particular ceilings. For regulated professions or activities, specific policies may be needed.

Prevention comes first

Insurance repairs damage, it does not prevent it. Simple habits reduce both risks: keep a written record of what was agreed with the client, confirm important deliveries in writing, back up work files, and respect safety rules on site, including using the protective equipment provided. For physical work, ask for the site safety information before starting. Prevention protects you first, and it also makes any later insurance claim far easier to document and defend.

Frequently asked questions

Is professional liability insurance included?

Yes, professional liability insurance is included in the members' package, within the limits, ceilings and exclusions stated in the policy.

Does INAIL cover people working from home?

INAIL cover depends on the type of relationship and the activities performed. It is provided for employment relationships; conditions for remote work must be checked on the case.

Can I see the policy?

Yes, we provide the policy conditions, with ceilings and exclusions, before you join.
