Foreign client paying late: who deals with it

Late payment is the most frequent problem for the self-employed, and with foreign clients it gets worse: time zones, different languages, slow internal procedures, distant accounting offices. For a freelancer it is a job within the job. With Tesla3000 the task moves from the person to the structure.

Why delays weigh more on the individual

When a professional invoices directly, they have to chase, often in a language that is not their own, an office they do not know. Each reminder takes time, and a tone that is too firm can damage the client relationship. Meanwhile personal expenses do not wait. An individual also has little bargaining power: a large client tends to impose its own timing.

With the cooperative the invoice is issued and chased by the structure, which has a direct commercial relationship, reminder procedures, traceable documents and knowledge of contract terms. The member carries on working without having to turn into a debt collector.

Example: Irene and the Amsterdam agency

Example: imagine Irene, a designer employed through the cooperative, working for an Amsterdam agency that pays at 60 days. At month end Irene receives her payslip as planned; the cooperative chases the agency when the invoice falls due and, if timing stretches, discusses it with the administrative office. Irene never wrote a single reminder email. In one case when an invoice stayed open longer than expected, the cooperative considered an advance through the fund so as not to weigh on members’ cash flow.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Payment terms in the engagement. Deadlines, late-payment penalties provided in the contract and payment methods are defined.
  2. Invoicing. The cooperative issues the invoice based on activities logged on the platform.
  3. Monitoring. The due date is followed by the structure, not by you.
  4. Reminder. If payment is late, the cooperative contacts the client’s administrative office.
  5. Advance. Where provided, an advance through the fund can be considered, on agreed conditions.
  6. Escalation. In harder cases the cooperative assesses the contractual actions available.

How pay is regulated

The member’s employment relationship provides pay under the contract and the internal regulation, which define how engagements with delayed receipts are treated. We cannot promise that no delay will ever affect your relationship: that is why, before starting, we explain clearly what the regulation provides and how it applies to the engagement you are interested in. It is a conversation worth having at the beginning, not when a delay is already under way.

What you can do

Even though reminders are the cooperative’s job, some behaviours help:

  • log activities and attendance on the platform promptly;
  • obtain written confirmation of deliveries and approvals from the client;
  • report to the cooperative at once any signs of trouble, such as unanswered messages or disputes;
  • make no side agreements with the client on fees or deadlines, because the contract belongs to the cooperative.

Limits and transparency

No structure can guarantee that a client always pays on time. If a client is insolvent or raises a well-founded dispute, the cooperative must assess the consequences and the member must be informed. For VAT on invoices abroad each case is assessed (reverse charge for EU B2B services; normally not subject to Italian VAT for non-EU).

Choosing clients with payment in mind

Prevention is better than recovery. Before accepting an engagement, ask the client about payment terms and how invoices are approved, and look at how they have behaved with other suppliers if that information is available. A large client paying slowly by habit is a different risk from a small one in difficulty. The cooperative can help assess the offer, suggest an advance for part of the fee or ask for a deposit, so that the work starts on a balanced footing.

Frequently asked questions

If the client does not pay, do I still receive my pay?

The employment relationship provides pay under the contract. Conditions linked to engagements with delayed receipts are set out in the regulation and contract, and we explain them before you start.

What is an advance through the fund?

It is a way of advancing payment through a cooperative fund, useful when the client pays slowly. Conditions are agreed case by case.

Who chases the client?

The cooperative, which holds the commercial contract and the invoice.
