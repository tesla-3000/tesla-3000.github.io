UX researcher for a Dutch software house

Dutch software houses are used to working with professionals across Europe and value research highly: interviews, usability tests, card sorting, diary studies. For an Italian UX researcher the typical engagement is a project of a few months, with deliverables that shape the product. Tesla3000 handles the contractual and administrative side.

What you actually deliver

Research deliverables are clear and measurable: research plan, interview guides, recordings and transcripts, synthesis of findings (insights), personas, journey maps, usability reports with issue severity, recommendations. Dutch teams appreciate directness and concreteness: few slides, clear results tied to product decisions. Agree in the contract:

  • number of sessions and participant profiles;
  • who recruits and who pays participants;
  • deliverable format and deadlines;
  • tools (Figma, Miro, Dovetail, Maze) and their licences.

How the cooperative fits

The cooperative signs the agreement with the software house and invoices. For B2B services to the EU the reverse charge generally applies (art. 7-ter of decree 633/72), checked case by case. As a worker-member (Italian Law 142/2001) you have a work relationship with the cooperative: if employment, INPS contributions, INAIL, paid leave, sick leave, severance and a payslip. Costs (tools, travel, participant incentives if provided for) go through expense reports.

Typical pitfalls

  • Consent and privacy. Recording an interview requires informed consent and GDPR care: name the controller and processor in the agreement.
  • Slow recruitment. Finding qualified participants takes time: estimate the days.
  • Findings not used. Schedule a readout with the team.
  • Research versus design. If UI work is also requested, widen the agreement.
  • Time zone and language. Interviews in Italian for an English product need translated verbatims.

Example

Example: imagine Sara, a UX researcher in Milan, hired by a software house in Utrecht to understand how Italian physiotherapists use an exercise app: 12 interviews and 2 usability sessions in eight weeks. The cooperative signs the agreement, covering 25 working days and a budget for participant incentives. Sara records days and expenses on the platform, delivers the report and presents findings to the team by video call. At month end she receives her payslip. Fictional character.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Intro call. You explain project, days, rate, tools.
  2. Joining. Share of capital, refundable on exit under the bylaws.
  3. Agreement. Commercial contract with the software house.
  4. Activity. Days, deliverables and expenses on the platform.
  5. Invoicing. The cooperative invoices and collects.
  6. Payslip. Monthly pay and documents online.

INAIL, professional liability insurance and WhatsApp/email support are included.

And with a VAT number?

The flat-rate regime (revenue up to 85,000 euro, 15% substitute tax, 5% for the first five years if conditions are met) works well for those with a broad client portfolio. For those working with few principals who want less admin, the cooperative deserves a comparison.

Checklist before signing

Before kickoff, ask for: the business goals of the research and the decisions that depend on it; participant selection criteria; the informed consent template and privacy notice; the budget for incentives and tools; the output format (slides, report, repository). Agree a readout date with product and design teams: research has value when it arrives before decisions. Allow margin for recruitment, the item that most often stretches timelines. Share the plan with us and we turn it into clauses of the agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run interviews with users in Italy for a Dutch client?

Yes. Recruitment, participant incentives and informed consent should be covered in the agreement; handling of personal data needs care.

Can participant incentives be reimbursed?

If included in the agreed budget and documented, they can be handled as expenses. Defined case by case.

Can I also work for other clients?

In general yes, within confidentiality and any exclusivity agreed in the contract.
