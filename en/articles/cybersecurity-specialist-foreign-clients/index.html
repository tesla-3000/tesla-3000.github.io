Cybersecurity specialist for foreign clients: assignments and liability

A Singapore fintech needs a penetration test on its APIs, a Dublin law firm wants its cloud configuration checked, a German manufacturer seeks an analyst for a hardening period after an incident. Cybersecurity is a field where skills are scarce and many Italian professionals are in demand from foreign companies. But it is also a field where contractual liability weighs far more than elsewhere.

Types of assignment

  • Penetration tests on web, mobile and infrastructure: one to three weeks, with a final report and retest.
  • Configuration reviews (cloud, identity, endpoints) with prioritised recommendations.
  • SOC support or monitoring, often in shifts or on call.
  • Incident response with urgent activity and higher fees.
  • Compliance consulting, such as preparing for information-security certifications.

Deliverables are reports with vulnerabilities ranked by severity, technical evidence, a remediation plan and a presentation for managers.

The contract matters more here

  1. Scope and authorisation: a written list of systems you may test, time windows and permitted methods. The client must authorise in writing.
  2. Confidentiality: vulnerabilities and data found must be handled with particular care, under non-disclosure clauses.
  3. Liability: limits and insurance cover must be clarified; testing production without agreements risks damage.
  4. Personal data: if you access personal data during the work, specific agreements with the client are needed.
  5. Disclosure: what you may say publicly afterwards, for example at a conference or in an article, and under what conditions.

Good operating practice

Always work from a dedicated environment, separate from your personal one, and keep logs of activities with date and time: they protect you in disputes. Exchange results only over encrypted channels agreed with the client and delete collected data within the agreed time. Before a test on production systems, get written confirmation from someone with decision power and a contact reachable during the activity. A clear report with priorities and remediation steps is worth more than a long list of vulnerabilities.

Example: a three-week test

A fictional example. Imagine Roberto, a penetration tester with recognised certifications and ten years’ experience. An Asian fintech commissions a three-week test on its payment APIs plus a retest two months later. Roberto has always worked as an employee and does not want a VAT number for a single assignment. Through the cooperative, Tesla3000 signs the contract with scope, confidentiality and liability limits; Roberto is engaged as a worker-member, logs test and reporting days on the platform and gets the monthly payslip; the client pays the cooperative in dollars on delivery of the report.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Tell us about the assignment: type of test, client, duration, contractual requirements.
  2. Membership with a share of capital refundable on exit (Law 142/2001).
  3. Contract: scope, confidentiality, liability, on-call.
  4. Operations on the platform: activities, days, documents, signing.
  5. Protections: INPS, INAIL, holidays, sick leave, severance pay; professional liability included in the package.
  6. Invoicing: the cooperative invoices the foreign client, with B2B rules verified case by case; advance possible.

VAT number, a comparison

A security consultant with many stable clients may prefer a VAT number under the flat-rate regime (85,000 euro threshold, 15% substitute tax or 5% for the first five years with requirements), with Separate Management contributions and full autonomy. It means handling invoices, returns and one’s own professional insurance. The cooperative works well for those working project by project for one client at a time and preferring that contract, insurance and admin be handled by a structure.

Frequently asked questions

Who is liable if a test damages the systems?

It depends on the contract and the authorised scope. The professional liability insurance included in the package helps, but limits must be checked and the scope of the test written precisely.

Can I use my own certifications and tools?

Certifications yes, they are yours. For tools under a personal licence, agree with the client what is used and how collected material is handled.

How is on-call duty for incidents handled?

It must be defined in the contract, with hours, extra pay and expected response times.
