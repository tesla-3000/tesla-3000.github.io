Musicians playing festivals abroad: contracts and fees

A musician playing European festivals has a patchwork calendar: three dates in Germany in July, a week in France in August, a club residency in Lisbon. Each booking comes with a different promoter, fee and paperwork. Tesla3000 offers a stable structure that brings these gigs into a single employment relationship.

How a booking is structured

Festival contracts usually state date, set time, gross fee, any travel and accommodation reimbursement, technical rider and payment terms. Many promoters pay after the event, sometimes at 30 days; others ask for a deposit to hold the date. If the band has several members, you need to decide who receives the payment and how it is split.

The cooperative’s role

The cooperative signs the commercial contract with the promoter, invoices the fee and collects. As a worker-member (Italian Law 142/2001) you have a work relationship with the cooperative, employment or another type depending on the case. You receive a payslip with contributions and INAIL cover, and where applicable paid leave, sick leave and severance accrue. Important: live performance has specific social security and contractual rules; the classification must be checked with us before you start. Copyright or neighbouring rights on performances also have their own regime and remain to be verified.

Typical pitfalls

  • Cash or off-the-books fees. Avoid them: they leave you unprotected.
  • Undocumented expenses. Keep tickets and receipts.
  • Cancellations. Agree what happens if the festival cancels.
  • Gear and rentals. Clarify who pays transport and backline.
  • Multiple countries. Social security and tax depend on destination and duration: case by case.

Example

Example: imagine Davide, a jazz guitarist from Perugia with three summer bookings: two festivals in Germany and a club in Lisbon, seven evenings in all. The cooperative signs three agreements with the promoters. Davide logs the evenings on the platform and uploads train and hotel expenses; the cooperative invoices the fees, collects and then runs his payslip. The payment advance fund spares him the 30-day wait when rent is due. Character and details are fictional.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Calendar. You send your bookings with dates, venues and fees.
  2. Joining. You subscribe a share of capital, refundable on exit under the bylaws.
  3. Agreements. The cooperative signs with each promoter.
  4. Activity. Attendance and expenses on the platform.
  5. Collection. The cooperative invoices and collects, also in foreign currency.
  6. Payslip. You receive pay with payslip and documents on the platform.

INAIL cover, professional liability insurance and WhatsApp support are included.

When something else fits better

If your gigs are few and occasional, a flat-rate VAT-number setup may fit (revenue up to 85,000 euro, 15% substitute tax, 5% for the first five years if conditions are met), with admin on your side. If you play often and want one continuous contribution position, the cooperative deserves a comparison.

Checklist before signing

Before confirming a date, check the contract with the promoter: gross or net fee, currency, who pays any local withholding tax, payment terms, travel and lodging reimbursement, backline and what happens in case of bad weather or cancellation. Always ask for the technical rider and rehearsal times. If you play in a non-EU country, find out in advance about visas and work permits for performers. Keep a shared calendar with confirmed dates and those under negotiation: it helps us plan payslips and advances without surprises. Send us the contracts before you sign them.

Frequently asked questions

Does the cooperative manage my royalties (SIAE)?

No: copyright and neighbouring rights follow their own rules. The cooperative manages the work relationship and the fee; rights should be checked case by case.

What if I perform in another country?

Social security, tax and permits depend on the country and the length of stay. Each tour is assessed case by case.

How are travel and accommodation reimbursed?

Through expense reports on the platform, if provided for in the agreement and documented.
