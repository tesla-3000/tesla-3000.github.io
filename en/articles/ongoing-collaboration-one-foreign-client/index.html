Use cases

Ongoing work for a single foreign client: what to do

It happens often: you start as a freelancer, find a satisfied foreign client and within a few months that client is almost all your work. Agreed hours, weekly meetings, shared tools. Formally you are a supplier; in practice you work like a stable team member. In this situation a VAT number can become an awkward form: all the risk is yours, and the client can stop whenever it wants.

What changes in a stable collaboration

With one client, your income depends on a single decision. Sickness, holidays, maternity or the end of the contract are not automatically covered for people who invoice. With Tesla3000 you can instead have an employment relationship, often as an employee, governed by Law 142/2001 and the cooperative’s internal regulation: INPS contributions paid by the cooperative, INAIL, holidays, sick leave, maternity and paternity leave, TFR severance pay and, if you lose work involuntarily, NASpI under the legal conditions.

The client does not have to change habits: it keeps working with you as before, but its contract is with the cooperative and it receives invoices from the cooperative.

Example: Luca and the German client

Example: imagine Luca, a technical translator who has worked almost exclusively for a Munich company for a year. He invoices monthly under his own VAT number and feels poorly protected: if he falls ill, income stops; if the client ends the relationship, he has no safety net. He decides to move into the cooperative.

The cooperative signs a commercial contract with the German company, setting the rate and delivery terms. Luca keeps working exactly as before, but now receives a monthly payslip and accrues holidays and TFR. If the client later reduces its orders, Luca can remain a member and look for other clients through the members’ community.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Review of the current relationship. You describe how you work today: hours, tools, goals.
  2. Membership. You subscribe the share capital quota and accept the internal regulation.
  3. New contract with the client. The cooperative offers the foreign client a commercial contract replacing your individual invoices.
  4. Employment contract. We define your relationship with the cooperative.
  5. Day-to-day. You log activities and attendance on the platform, download payslips and request expense reimbursements.
  6. Collection. We invoice and manage payments, including in foreign currency, with a possible advance through the fund.

Points to watch

The change must be explained well to the client: for them, the contract holder and the invoicing entity change, not the work. Some foreign companies like the arrangement because it simplifies supplier management and reduces the risk of misclassifying a contractor.

For VAT treatment of invoices to foreign clients (B2B services, reverse charge within the EU, not subject to Italian VAT outside the EU) we refer to a check on the individual case. If you live abroad, your tax residence and any double taxation treaties must be assessed separately.

Remember too that the rate is negotiable: the member in practice defines their own work and fee, and the cooperative formalises it in the engagement.

Mistakes to avoid

The first is continuing to invoice personally after the switch: once the contract is with the cooperative, individual invoices no longer make sense and should be closed in an orderly way. The second is not warning the client in time: it takes a few weeks to update supplier records, purchase orders and payment details. The third is assuming the terms stay identical: rate, hours and duration should be rewritten in the engagement, so everyone knows what was agreed.

When it makes sense

The model suits best when there is one client, the relationship is stable and you miss protections. If instead you work with many different clients and prefer full autonomy, a VAT number may remain a valid choice. To work out which case is yours, contact us.

Frequently asked questions

If I work for one foreign client, is a cooperative better than a VAT number?

It depends. With a single client economic dependence is high: an employment relationship with payslips offers protections a VAT number does not provide by itself. It must be assessed case by case.

Does the foreign client become my employer?

No. Your employer is the cooperative. The client signs a commercial contract with the cooperative and gives day-to-day direction on the project.

Can I change client without changing set-up?

Yes. You can stay a member and manage a new engagement with another client by setting up the relevant agreements.
