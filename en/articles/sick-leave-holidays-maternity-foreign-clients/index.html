Advantages

Sick leave, holidays and maternity when working for foreign clients

For a freelancer, stopping almost always means stopping earning. A week of flu, two of holiday or parental leave are periods when no invoice goes out. When the client is abroad, explaining an absence and renegotiating deliveries can be extra work. With Tesla3000, an employment relationship changes the picture.

Sick leave

In an employment relationship sick leave follows the law and the applicable contract: a medical certificate sent electronically, prompt notice to the cooperative, and any allowance under social security and contract rules. The concrete conditions depend on your classification and contract: we give no figures or durations here.

The practical advantage is that you notify the cooperative, not the foreign client. The cooperative, which holds the commercial contract, informs the client, works with you on reorganising deliveries and, if needed, proposes a substitute among members for urgent activities.

Holidays

A member with an employment relationship accrues holidays under law and contract. They are planned with the cooperative, which coordinates the calendar with the client. For those working with foreign companies, remember that public holidays differ by country: a shutdown in Sweden does not coincide with one in Spain. Agreeing absences early avoids surprises.

Maternity and paternity

For an employment relationship, maternity and paternity leave is provided under the law, with allowances from the social security system under legal conditions. It is one of the most important protections compared with self-employment. Precise rules (duration, requirements, amounts) should be checked on the actual case with the cooperative or an advice centre.

Example: Anna and the Barcelona client

Example: imagine Anna, a copywriter working for a Barcelona agency. Mid-project she learns she is expecting a baby. As a freelancer she would have had to decide alone how to handle the project and her income. In the cooperative she informs the members’ coordinator; the cooperative talks to the agency, redistributes some activities to another member and confirms Anna’s leave period under the applicable rules. The agency does not change its contractual counterpart.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Notification. You tell the cooperative as soon as possible (WhatsApp or email) of sickness, holidays or leave.
  2. Documents. You upload certificates and requests on the platform.
  3. Reorganisation. The cooperative reviews deliveries and priorities with you.
  4. Dialogue with the client. Handled by the cooperative, within the commercial contract.
  5. Payslip. Absences are reflected under the applicable rules.
  6. Return. You resume activities with the updated engagement.

What to do beforehand

Read the internal regulation and your employment contract carefully: they state notice periods and required documents. If you work from another country, ask how Italian rules coordinate with those of your country of residence. Do not assume protections are identical in every form of relationship: only employment carries this set of guarantees.

It also helps to agree with the client, already in the engagement, realistic delivery times and margins for unforeseen events: it is the best protection against the stress of a sudden absence.

The relationship with the client during your absence

For a foreign client an absence is an unknown until it receives clear information. A short message stating when deliveries resume and who the contact is in the meantime avoids tension. Since the commercial contract belongs to the cooperative, it keeps the dialogue going: you do not have to justify yourself or renegotiate in person, and in difficult moments that is a real relief. It still helps to update project colleagues on the state of the work before you step away.

Frequently asked questions

If I fall ill, does the foreign client stop paying?

In an employment relationship sick leave follows the law and the applicable contract. The relationship with the client belongs to the cooperative, which handles any delays.

Are holidays paid?

In an employment relationship holidays accrue under contract and law. They are agreed with the cooperative and communicated to the client in good time.

Do maternity and paternity leave apply?

Yes, statutory leave applies to an employment relationship. Conditions and allowances must be checked for your case.
