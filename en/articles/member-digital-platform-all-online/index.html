Advantages

The member's digital platform: everything online

People working for foreign clients often move between time zones, cities and countries. Having one place to find contracts, payslips and receipts avoids searching emails or folders. The digital platform of Tesla3000 is designed for that: everything concerning your relationship with the cooperative is online.

What you find on the platform

Engagements. The engagements you are assigned to, with client, period, planned activities and main conditions. You always know where you stand.

Activities and attendance. You log what you do, day by day or as the engagement requires. It is the basis for pay, client invoicing and time checks.

Expense claims. You upload receipts for trips and purchases, with dates and amounts, and follow the status of reimbursement.

Payslips. Each month you find your payslip, downloadable and archived. The same goes for the annual CU.

Documents and signing. Employment contract, regulation, notices, attestations: you consult them and, where provided, sign them online.

Communications. Notices from the cooperative, assembly invitations and updates.

Why it matters for remote workers

The platform reduces typical frictions of remote work: no paper signatures to post, no hunting for old documents, no waiting for a file by email. If you are travelling, in another time zone or another country, you can still reach your data. It also helps the foreign client: logged and approved activities are the basis of invoicing, with fewer misunderstandings.

Example: Teresa across three countries

Example: imagine Teresa, a translator who spends the year between Italy, Portugal and Germany. At the start of the month she logs hours for the current project, uploads a ticket receipt, signs the engagement addendum online and downloads last month’s payslip, which she needs for a rental contract. She did not have to print anything or post documents. For a doubt about the addendum she writes to support on WhatsApp.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Access. On joining you receive credentials and a short guide.
  2. Profile. You check your details and contact information.
  3. Engagement. You find assigned engagements and their conditions.
  4. Daily use. You log activities, attendance and expenses.
  5. Documents. You consult payslips, CU and contracts, and sign where required.
  6. Support. If you have trouble, you write to support via WhatsApp or email.

Good habits

Logging activities regularly, for example at the end of the day, avoids omissions and simplifies invoicing. Check from time to time that your records are up to date, particularly contact and payment details. Keep a copy of important documents outside the platform too. Use strong passwords and, if available, two-factor authentication.

Limits to know

A platform is a tool: it does not replace human discussion for complex matters. There may be maintenance periods or malfunctions: in that case support tells you how to proceed. Personal data is processed in compliance with the law; details are in the notice you receive when joining.

Leaving a clear trail

A less obvious advantage of working online is traceability. Every logged activity, uploaded receipt and signed document leaves a dated record. If a client queries a delivery, if you need to prove what was agreed, or if an authority asks for documents, the answer is a few clicks away instead of a search through old emails. For a professional who works alone, that orderly archive is a form of protection, and it costs only a few minutes of regular habit each week.

If you change device or computer, remember to log out of sessions left open on devices you no longer use: a simple step that protects your documents.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to install an app?

Access details (web or app) are given when you join. We guide you through your first login.

Can I sign documents online?

Yes. The platform supports signing documents related to the relationship, under the methods and legal value provided by applicable law.

Is my data protected?

Personal data is processed under data protection law; information is provided in the cooperative's privacy notice.
