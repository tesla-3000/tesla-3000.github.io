Ghostwriter for foreign authors: confidentiality, rights and pay

An American entrepreneur has a story to tell but no time or craft to write it. An Australian consultant wants a book on her leadership method. A German scientist wants a popular version of his work. The ghostwriter is the person who gives voice to these stories without appearing on the cover. For an Italian professional who writes well in English or other languages, it is an international market, but a job with a particular kind of contract.

How a ghostwriting project runs

  1. Initial interviews: recordings, conversations, existing materials.
  2. Outline and sample: book structure and a trial chapter.
  3. Drafting by chapter, with author review after each.
  4. Overall revision, consistency of voice and fact-checking.
  5. Final delivery of the manuscript, sometimes with help in dealing with the publisher.

A book of 50,000-70,000 words generally takes several months. Payment is almost always in stages: an instalment on signing, one per chapter or at mid-manuscript, the balance on delivery.

Contract hinge points

  • Confidentiality: the relationship itself is confidential. No mention without permission.
  • Assignment of rights: clarify that the text belongs to the author and what stays with the ghostwriter (methods, generic structures).
  • Revisions: rounds included per chapter; beyond that, a separate rate.
  • Approval times: if the author delays feedback, delivery deadlines slip accordingly.
  • If the book is never published: your fee should not depend on sales.

Protecting the relationship with the author

Ghostwriting is a trust job. Record interview sessions with the author’s consent, keep dated chapter versions with comments, and agree on one person who approves texts, even if assistants or editors are involved. Set a realistic calendar that accounts for the author’s commitments. Settle the tone at the start: a first-person book needs a recognisable voice, and the initial sample exists precisely to check that the author recognises themselves in the pages.

Example: a leadership handbook

A fictional example. Imagine Marco, a writer with editorial experience, receiving an assignment from a British executive: a 60,000-word book in eight months, with four payment stages. Marco does not want invoices in his own name because the contract requires strict confidentiality, and he wants a stable relationship. Through the cooperative, Tesla3000 signs the contract with the client, including a confidentiality clause, and Marco, as a worker-member, logs interview and writing hours on the platform. The monthly payslip arrives even in months with no payment stage, thanks to an advance from the fund.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Contact: tell us the assignment and conditions.
  2. Check: for assignments of rights and creative works the rules are specific and we check them case by case.
  3. Membership with a share of capital refundable on exit (Law 142/2001).
  4. Contract with stages, confidentiality, assignment of rights, revisions.
  5. Platform: hours worked, expenses for travel and transcripts, signed documents.
  6. Pay: payslip with contributions, INAIL and professional liability included; invoicing the client in foreign currency too under B2B rules verified case by case.

A comparison with the VAT number

In ghostwriting, working under the flat-rate regime (85,000 euro threshold, 15% substitute tax, 5% for the first five years with requirements) is very common: you invoice per stage and pay Separate Management contributions. It is straightforward if you have few projects a year and prefer full autonomy. In the cooperative, stage payments become a regular monthly payslip, with holidays, sick leave and severance pay, useful on long projects.

Frequently asked questions

Does my name appear on the book?

Normally not: the byline stays with the author. An acknowledgement or mention can be agreed. It is a contractual choice.

How are copyright matters settled?

In ghostwriting, economic rights are usually assigned to the client. Copyright rules are specific and must be checked case by case.

Can I mention the project in my portfolio?

Only if the confidentiality agreement allows it. Often the work cannot be cited.
