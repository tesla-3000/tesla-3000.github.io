Advantages

No accountant and no personal bookkeeping

Many professionals start working out of passion and discover after a few months that a large part of their time goes to paper: electronic invoices, ledgers, settlements, payment forms, notices, requests from the accountant. Working as a member of Tesla3000 moves much of this load off your desk.

What you no longer do

With an employment relationship in the cooperative you do not open a VAT number, so:

  • you do not issue invoices to clients: the cooperative does;
  • you keep no ledgers or accounts and file no VAT returns;
  • you do not pay contributions yourself: INPS is handled by the cooperative, with the worker’s share withheld from the payslip;
  • you do not chase clients for unpaid invoices;
  • you do not choose invoicing software or pay for a subscription.

The relationship with the foreign client is contracted by the cooperative, which also handles VAT treatment of invoices, assessed case by case (normally B2B services to EU clients under reverse charge pursuant to Article 7-ter of Presidential Decree 633/72; to non-EU clients, transactions generally not subject to Italian VAT).

What remains your responsibility

Honesty matters: not everything disappears. Your personal tax situation remains. Employment income is subject to progressive income tax, with withholding at source and year-end adjustment, plus regional and municipal surtaxes. You receive a CU, which you can use for your tax return when required. If you have other income or particular deductions, you can turn to a tax assistance centre or a personal adviser. Organising your work also remains: logging activities and attendance on the platform and uploading expense receipts.

Example: Stefania and the end of Wednesday evenings

Example: imagine Stefania, a communications consultant. For years she spent every Wednesday evening on invoices, deadlines and meetings with her accountant. Since becoming a member with an employment relationship, Wednesday evening is free. She logs her activities on the platform, uploads travel receipts and at month end finds her payslip. When tax-return season arrives she uses the CU with a tax assistance centre for her personal return.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Closing the VAT number. If you already have one, we explain how to close it in an orderly way; you handle the procedure with your accountant.
  2. Membership. You join the cooperative with the share capital quota.
  3. Contract with the client. The cooperative signs it; from then on it invoices.
  4. Day to day. Activities, attendance and expense claims on the platform.
  5. Payslip. Each month, with withholdings and contributions already calculated.
  6. Annual CU. At the start of the year, for your personal return.

Beware of transition periods

If you are moving from an existing VAT number, there are pending invoices, advance payments and closing formalities: they must not be forgotten. Closing the old position properly is an important step and your current accountant is the right person to do it with. Consider also the impact on any Italian clients still receiving your invoices: you need an agreement on how to continue, through the cooperative.

Anyone living abroad must also have tax residence and double-taxation treaties checked: we cannot take it for granted.

The hidden cost of paperwork

Besides time, paperwork has a cost: the accountant’s fee, invoicing software, mistakes leading to penalties or late-payment corrections. With the cooperative these management costs are included in the service and you do not bear them directly as a professional. Of course the model must still be assessed as a whole, because the cooperative involves a share capital quota and conditions set out in its regulation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I still have to file a tax return?

It depends on your personal situation. With the CU from the cooperative you can use the pre-filled 730 or a tax assistance centre, but the obligation and method must be checked on your case.

Who handles VAT on invoices to foreign clients?

The cooperative, as the invoicing party. VAT treatment is assessed case by case.

What if I have other income?

Other income, such as rent or other activities, must still be declared under the rules. The cooperative handles only its own relationship with you.
