Advantages

Training and member community: you don't have to work alone

Working remotely for a foreign client has many advantages, but also a hidden cost: you often work alone. No colleague at the coffee machine, nobody to ask “how would you have handled this?”. A worker cooperative exists precisely to overcome that limit, and at Tesla3000 training and community are part of the service, not an add-on.

Why community matters more than it seems

People who work independently run into the same problems: how to structure an estimate, how to handle a request outside the agreed scope, how to organise the day across time zones. Talking to other members who have faced the same situation saves time and mistakes.

The member community is a place to share:

  • experience with clients in different countries and ways of working;
  • tools and methods for staying organised;
  • practical questions about administration, timesheets and expense claims;
  • opportunities and complementary skills.

As a member of a cooperative, you are not in a client-supplier relationship: it is a community of people with shared interests, in line with the mutual-benefit spirit of Italian cooperative law.

Training: what to expect

Training has two sides. One covers the basics of the employment relationship: workplace safety, correct use of the platform, rules on timesheets and reporting. The other covers professional growth: communicating with international clients, time management, languages, digital tools for your field.

Some training may be mandatory by law, especially on safety; for the rest, it follows the member’s interest. The actual programme depends on the period and on members’ needs, so always check it with the cooperative.

Example: Luca, web developer

An example, purely fictional: Luca is a web developer working for a software company based in Germany. After a few months he notices he keeps discussing the same issues with the client: time estimates and changes mid-project. In the community he asks how other members dealt with similar cases, gets two different approaches and a template for an estimate document. He also follows a short course on English communication in remote projects. His contract does not change, but the way he works does.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Access to the members’ channel. After admission, you are added to the discussion spaces the cooperative indicates.
  2. Tell us your interests. You let support know which topics matter to you, so you get the right invitations.
  3. Free participation. Meetings and training materials follow a calendar; you join when it is useful.
  4. Requests for new topics. Members can suggest subjects: training is also built bottom-up.
  5. Daily support. For operational questions, WhatsApp and email remain available, together with the digital platform.

What to check

  • Which courses are mandatory and which optional for your activity.
  • Whether training counts as working time: this should be clarified in your employment contract and the internal regulation.
  • What materials and meetings are available in your language.

An advantage that builds over time

The value of a community is not visible on day one. It shows after a year, when you have a network of colleagues to ask for advice, ready-made materials, and a more orderly path of growth than you would have improvised alone. For people working with foreign clients, where cultural and contractual differences are frequent, this practical support is the difference between feeling isolated and feeling part of something.

Frequently asked questions

Is training mandatory for members?

It depends on the type of work and the internal regulation. Some courses, such as workplace safety, may be required by law; the rest are optional professional development.

Can I find clients through the community?

The community is mainly for sharing experience and skills. Referrals between members can happen, but they should not be treated as a guarantee of work.

Do I have to attend every meeting?

No, participation is voluntary. If you work different hours or in another time zone, you can use the written channels and recordings where available.
