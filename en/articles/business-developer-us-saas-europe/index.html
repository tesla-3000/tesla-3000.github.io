Business developer for a US SaaS in Europe: the right contract

A software house in San Francisco wants to enter Italy, France and Spain but has no branch and no European staff. It needs a business developer who speaks the buyers’ language, can open conversations with companies and prepares the ground for the first contracts. For the professional it is an opportunity, but also an ambiguous area: not an employee, not exactly an agent, and the American company does not know how to pay them.

What a market-entry business developer really does

In the first twelve months of a European expansion the job is very different from that of an account executive in a mature market:

  • market mapping: lists of target companies, decision-makers, technology stack;
  • multichannel outreach: email, LinkedIn, phone, industry events;
  • lead qualification with an agreed method (for example budget and urgency criteria);
  • demos together with an American sales engineer, often in awkward time zones;
  • product feedback: localisation, European compliance requirements, missing integrations.

So the metrics are qualified meetings, open opportunities and, only later, closed deals. A contract paying only on closing may leave the professional without income for months.

Pay structure to negotiate

The most balanced pattern is a fixed part tied to activity (days or monthly fee) plus a variable part on targets: qualified opportunities or recurring revenue collected. Points to clarify: what happens on renewals, how long after the last contact a customer still counts as “yours”, whether variable payments survive the end of the relationship.

Example: six months to open Italy

A fictional example. Imagine Davide, with eight years in business software. An American order-management SaaS offers him six months, with the goal of opening twenty qualified conversations with Italian distributors. Fixed monthly fee plus a bonus for each pilot started. Davide does not want a VAT number for a fixed-term contract, and the client cannot hire him in Italy. The cooperative signs the contract, Davide becomes a worker-member, logs activities and meetings, and the bonus is included in the payslip calculation as provided by the internal regulation.

Before signing: three questions

  • Who answers leads after the first meeting? Without a European account executive, the business developer may end up running negotiations without it being in the pay.
  • How quickly does the client give feedback on localised sales material?
  • How is performance judged in months when the sales cycle is long? A target based only on closings in six months is unrealistic in B2B.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. We define the assignment: targets, duration, fixed and variable parts, territories.
  2. Membership with a share of capital (refundable under the by-laws) and a separate employment relationship (Law 142/2001).
  3. Contract with the American client: Tesla3000 signs it and invoices the fee, in dollars too, with the B2B rules for non-EU services verified case by case.
  4. Platform: you log activities, trip and trade-fair expenses, documents.
  5. Monthly payslip with INPS, INAIL and professional liability included; an advance is available in months when the client pays at 60 days.
  6. End or renewal: at the end you receive severance pay and documents; you can extend with a new contract.

Compared with a VAT number

Under the flat-rate regime (85,000 euro threshold, 15% substitute tax, 5% in the first five years with requirements) you have maximum autonomy and can take on several clients, but you bear Separate Management contributions, dollar invoicing and the collection cycle yourself. Selling on behalf of third parties under a stable mandate as a commercial agent is a field with its own rules, including Enasarco, to be verified. In the cooperative the relationship is an employment one with ordinary protections, though your activity stays tied to the contract.

Frequently asked questions

Can I earn commission on sales?

Yes, a variable component can be set in the contract and recognised to the member under the internal regulation. Rules for commercial agents with a mandate (Enasarco) are specific and need checking.

Does the American SaaS need to open a company in Italy?

No, the client signs a commercial contract with the cooperative. For any obligations of the foreign company in Italy, a case-by-case check is advisable.

Who owns the contact list I build?

It depends on the contract. State in writing whether leads and the CRM belong to the client and what the member may reuse.
