Use cases

SEO specialist with clients in several countries

An experienced SEO specialist rarely has just one client: a travel site in France, a SaaS in Germany, an e-commerce shop in the UK. More clients mean more currencies, contracts, invoices and different deadlines. Tesla3000 lets you bring it all into a single work relationship, with separate agreements for each client.

How multi-client SEO work runs

SEO projects rest on monthly retainers or fixed-term projects: technical audit, keyword research, content plan, on-page optimisation, link building, monthly report. Each client has a different dashboard (Search Console, Analytics, tools like Semrush or Ahrefs) and a different pace. The real hidden cost is non-billable work: onboarding, calls, access, ad hoc requests. That is why the retainer should list included activities and hours.

For each client, agree:

  • included activities and estimated monthly hours;
  • access to sites and tools, and who pays for licences;
  • report frequency and format;
  • limits: no ranking guarantees.

The cooperative’s role

Each client signs an agreement with the cooperative, which invoices each one. For EU clients the reverse charge normally applies (art. 7-ter of decree 633/72); for non-EU clients generic B2B services are normally outside Italian VAT: checked case by case. As a worker-member (Italian Law 142/2001) you have a single work relationship with the cooperative: INPS contributions, INAIL and, if employment, paid leave, sick leave, severance and one monthly payslip, however many clients you have. Foreign-currency collections are handled by the cooperative.

Typical pitfalls

  • Impossible guarantees. A client demanding page one: explain the limits.
  • Unapproved content. On-page changes depend on the client: slow approvals slow results.
  • Scope creep. From SEO to CRO, content marketing, PR: redefine the agreement.
  • Algorithm updates. Results can swing: the report should explain changes, not hide them.
  • Competing clients. Consider conflicts of interest and confidentiality.

Example

Example: imagine Valentina, an SEO specialist in Florence, serving three clients: a travel site in Lyon, a SaaS in Berlin and a furniture e-commerce in Manchester. Each has its own retainer, in euro and pounds. The cooperative signs three agreements and invoices each; Valentina logs hours per client and receives a single monthly payslip. A client paying at 60 days does not stall her income thanks to the advance fund. Fictional character.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Intro call. You list clients, retainers, currencies, hours.
  2. Joining. Share of capital, refundable on exit under the bylaws.
  3. Agreements. One contract per client.
  4. Activity. Hours and reports per client on the platform.
  5. Invoicing. The cooperative invoices and collects, also in foreign currency.
  6. Payslip. One monthly pay with documents online.

INAIL, professional liability insurance and WhatsApp/email support are included.

Compared with a VAT number

With many clients the flat-rate regime (revenue up to 85,000 euro, 15% substitute tax, 5% for the first five years if conditions are met) is often convenient and leaves you fully autonomous. If you exceed the ceiling or do not want to handle invoicing and contributions, the cooperative is an alternative to weigh calmly.

Checklist before signing

With multiple clients in multiple countries, organise the work with a method: a sheet per client with planned and actual hours; a deadline calendar for audits and reports; a standard onboarding template; a rule for out-of-retainer requests. Check market language: SEO in French, German or British English needs native keyword research, not translations. Avoid concentrating all income on one client: losing a contract hurts less when the portfolio is balanced. Once a quarter, review profitability and workload for each client.

Frequently asked questions

How are clients in different currencies handled?

The cooperative can invoice and collect in foreign currency. Your pay on the payslip is in euro and conversion timing is agreed.

Can I guarantee rankings?

No: SEO results depend on many factors. The agreement describes activities and reporting, with no ranking promises.

How many clients can I serve?

There is no fixed limit: each client has an agreement and the workload must be compatible with your employment contract.
