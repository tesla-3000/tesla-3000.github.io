Photographer for international magazines: work and rights

A travel magazine in Amsterdam wants a feature on the Amalfi coast; a German architecture magazine wants shots of Venice; a London agency orders a harvest reportage. The Italian photographer has the places and the eye, but each title has its own rules on fees, expenses and rights. Tesla3000 helps turn scattered assignments into an orderly work relationship.

How editorial assignments work

Assignments fall into three types. Commissioned: the magazine orders a feature with a brief, working days and a fee. Commissioned with expenses: travel, lodging and an assistant are covered by the title, but must be documented. Archive purchase: the magazine buys a licence to use existing photos. Payment often comes after publication, sometimes at 60 days, and sometimes when the issue is out. The contract should state: number of photos delivered, format, uses (print, web, social), duration and territories.

Copyright: take care

Photographs are protected works. Assignment or licence, duration, scope and credit (byline) follow specific rules and are not automatically covered by the work relationship. The agreement defines what is granted to the client; each case deserves a check before accepting. The cooperative cannot guarantee the same formula for everyone.

What the cooperative does

The cooperative signs the agreement with the title, invoices the fee (for EU clients normally under reverse charge, art. 7-ter of decree 633/72; for non-EU clients as B2B services outside Italian VAT, to be verified) and collects, also in foreign currency. The worker-member (Italian Law 142/2001) has a work relationship with contributions, INAIL, and if employment, paid leave, sick leave and severance, plus a monthly payslip. Travel costs go through expense reports. No VAT number is needed.

Typical pitfalls

  • Payment on publication. If the title postpones the issue, payment slips too: an advance from the fund can help.
  • Releases. Recognisable people or private places need releases.
  • Equipment. Insurance and transport of gear should be clarified.
  • Unwritten reuse. Avoid the unwritten “can we use them for something else too?”
  • Retouching. What is included and when it is billed separately.

Example

Example: imagine Paolo, a photographer from Naples, commissioned by a Dutch travel magazine for a five-day feature on the Amalfi coast. The agreement covers 40 selected photos, a print and web licence for 24 months, and reimbursement of meals and lodging. Paolo logs the days on the platform, uploads receipts as expenses and delivers the photos. The cooperative invoices the magazine; Paolo receives his payslip. Fictional character.

How it works with the cooperative

  1. Brief. You send the assignment, fee and rights requested.
  2. Joining. Share of capital, refundable on exit under the bylaws.
  3. Agreement. Contract with the title, with clauses on rights and use.
  4. Activity. Days, expenses and deliveries on the platform.
  5. Collection. The cooperative invoices and collects.
  6. Payslip. Monthly pay with documents online.

INAIL and professional liability insurance are included, with WhatsApp/email support.

Compared with a VAT number

The flat-rate regime (revenue up to 85,000 euro, 15% substitute tax, 5% for the first five years if conditions are met) is widely used by photographers: full control and low costs, but contributions and paperwork are yours. The cooperative suits those who want worker protections and less bureaucracy.

Checklist before signing

Before shooting, get in writing: the purpose of the feature and planned uses; the number of images to deliver and the date; any releases required; reimbursement of expenses, assistant and rentals; the procedure for archiving and delivering files (RAW, JPEG, metadata). Note the credits and captions the title requires. Keep a log of days and expenses with receipts: it simplifies expense reports and invoicing. For private locations or events, check permits and accreditations with the client.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns the rights to my photographs?

Copyright in photographs has specific rules. Assignment or licensing is settled in the contract and should be checked case by case before you start.

How do travel expense reimbursements work?

If provided for in the agreement and documented, they go through expense reports on the platform.

Can I sell the same photos to others?

It depends on the exclusivity and licences agreed. Define them in writing beforehand.
